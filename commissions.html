Access Commission and Rewards Information

36 months of historical commission statements are available to principal agents on business.kp.org. Both PDF and CSV formats are supported. Commission processing dates are as follows:

  • CA, MAS, NW, & WA: 16th of the month
  • CO, GA, & HI: 23rd of the month
  • CA Rewards and Bonuses: 27th of the month

Where can I access commission information?

Navigate to business.kp.org and log in to your account. Once logged in, click on the "Broker Compensation" tab to view your commission statements. You can filter the statements by date range and download them in PDF or CSV format for your records.
