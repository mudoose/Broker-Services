Broker of Record Change Requirements

Broker of Record changes are effective based on the date the request was sent to Kaiser Permanente:

  • CA- If received by the first 5 calendar days, the request will be effetive the 1st of the current month.
  • CO- If Recevied by the first 3 calendar days, the request will be effetive the 1st of the current month.
  • GA, HI, & NW: If received by the first 15 calendar days, the request will be effetive the 1st of the current month.
  • MAS: Always effective the 1st of the month following 30 days from the date of receipt.

Who will sign the request?

Broker of Record change requests must always be signed by the customer client.

Do you offer self-service options?

Yes! Broker of Record changes can be processed systematically at business.kp.org. (Both Agency & Agent changes)

What information is needed?

A signed letter from the customer client that includes the following information:

  • Effective Date of Change
  • Customer Name & Customer ID
  • Broker Name and ID
  • Letter contains the customer company letterhead
  • Letter is dated
  • Statement of work indicates a broker of record change
  • Signatory field contains the Name & title of authorized company client representative
