Broker and Agent Appointment Process

Brokers must provide the following prior to being eligible for commission payments:

  • Be licensed in each state they will sell, renew, and/or market customers
  • Sign a broker agreement
  • Provide payables documentation (W-9, Demographic Information, etc.)
  • Register on business.kp.org to access online self-service tools and book of business.

Who is this for?

Broker Agencies and Agents whom have not yet been appointed with Kaiser Permanente.

How do I start?

Register for an online account at: business.kp.org and Select Appointment Type (Agency or Agent)

What information is needed?

Demographic and contact information (W9 Tax ID, License Details, Mailing Address, etc.). In addition, you will need to Sign a Broker Agreement & Business Associate Agreements !

Next Steps

A welcome letter with a Kaiser Permanente Broker ID will be emailed to you upon successful agency appointment completion.
